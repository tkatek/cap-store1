OUR STORY

Born on
the streets.
Built for
the world.

From a small workshop to a global movement.

01 / 15
2010
The Beginning

Two friends, one sewing machine, and a vision to create headwear that actually meant something.

2011
First Sample

After 60 failed prototypes, the first VEXO cap finally held its shape. We wore it everywhere.

2012
Street Hustle

Sold from the back of a van at local markets. Zero budget, maximum belief.

2013
First Store

Opened our flagship in downtown LA. 200 caps sold on day one. We knew this was real.

2014
Online Launch

Our website crashed on launch day. 4,000 orders in 2 hours. We called it a win.

2015
Media Moment

Featured in Highsnobiety. Our inbox exploded overnight. The culture noticed.

2016
Global Drop

Our limited collab with underground artists sold out in 4 minutes. 80,000 on the waitlist.

2017
10 Countries

Expanded to Europe and Asia. Our caps were spotted from Tokyo to Berlin in the same week.

2018
Studio Built

Moved into our own 8,000 sq ft studio. Real machines. Real team. Real craft.

2019
Sustainable Era

Switched to 100% recycled materials. Style without compromise. Planet first, always.

2020
Pivot & Persist

The world paused. We kept creating. Released 3 collections digitally and never missed a drop.

2021
1M Community

Crossed 1 million followers. Not vanity — a real community of cap obsessives worldwide.

2022
Award Winner

Best Sustainable Fashion Brand, Global Style Awards. We cried. Then went back to work.

2023
40 Countries

Now stocked in 200+ retailers across 40 countries. The vision finally matched the map.

2025
The Future

200+ styles. New materials lab. And a new chapter that's only just beginning. Welcome to VEXO.

HOW WE MAKE THEM

The Craft Behind Every Cap

01

Design

Every cap starts as a sketch. Our designers obsess over curves, proportions, and fabric flow for weeks.

02

Material

Sourced from certified sustainable mills. We touch, test, and tension every fabric before it cuts.

03

Craft

Hand-stitched panels. Precision-cut brims. A single cap takes 47 individual steps to complete.

04

QC

Each cap is inspected 3 times before shipping. If it's not perfect, it doesn't leave the studio.
